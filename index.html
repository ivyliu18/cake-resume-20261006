What I noticed · The gap

AI can see the problem. It can't see the person.

Cake's AI Resume Health Check does two things well every day: score a resume, flag its weaknesses. But after diagnosis, there is no next step. That's not my guess, it's Cake's own product language.

Set beside AmazingTalker, which does the same "diagnosis" job, a human shows up immediately afterward. That contrast is the gap. It's also the single thing Part 2 and Part 3 are both going to respond to.

One sentence to carry forward: the diagnosis is not the problem. The empty space right after it is.
這頁是整場簡報的「核心句」,建議背到可以不看稿講出來。講完停頓,給一點沉默的重量,再翻到下一頁接 Part2 的邏輯銜接。

Part 3 · Quantum Leap: Core Argument

The same gap, at company scale

The bridge already named it: diagnosis with no next step, set against AmazingTalker's human-first model. One more layer makes it urgent: recruiter contingency fees (15–25% of first-year salary, ~20% typical, ≈NT$150,000–300,000 per placement) structurally exclude entry-level and career-changing candidates: Cake's largest, least-monetized user segment.

The mechanism: use health-check weakness scoring to route people into a coach-matching, revenue-share marketplace, instead of leaving them to figure it out alone.
這頁是 Part3 開場,語氣要穩重。獵頭抽成那段數字可以講得篤定,這是業界常見基準。
Part 3 · Strategic Fit & Target Audience

Who this is for, and why it's provable

Entry-level and career-changers: not who recruiters chase directly, and not a new acquisition cost. These are users Cake already has; this is a second pass at value already paid for.

Risk if ignored: quiet drift off Cake AI diagnosis done → add the human layer
1International precedent
Uber proved a P2P match + commission model scales without owning the supply side; BetterUp and Coaching.com proved people already pay for matched career coaching, inside the typical 20–30% P2P marketplace take rate.
2Local precedent
AmazingTalker already runs this exact UX in Taiwan: tutor-set pricing, coach-matching flow, now a career-coaching category. A mechanism reference, not Cake's own take rate.
3How Cake pushes it
Three-phase roadmap: 2-week concierge test → semi-automated + international tiers → full marketplace. Detail next.
4How it doesn't become the main line
Stays an add-on to the resume platform, not a rebrand. Owned by Growth Ops validation, no new department. Public story stays "job platform."
5New partner channel
Mandarin-language schools & education providers can take the same shape as Cake's Campus Ambassador programme: a coach-supply channel and an inbound channel to overseas Chinese-speaking job seekers.
6Cost & execution
NT$75K Phase 1 cost, designed to break even, zero engineering spend, two-week test, detailed on the next few slides.
這頁是合併過的新頁,前身是兩頁(「為什麼是這群人」+「六點論證」),合併成一頁減少翻頁停頓。開場先講受眾:entry 使用者不是獵頭會主動 reach out 的對象,找工作沒成功也不一定會留在 Cake 上——這是留存風險,不是獵頭排除的老論點重講。「從現有這群使用者出發、再次創造價值,就能證明沒有浪費」這句要講得篤定、慢一點,這是面試官最容易問「為什麼是這群人」的地方。AI 診斷已經做完、下一步是導入真人這句帶過就好,不用展開 Uber 類比(類比已經收進第1點)。接著六點論證:第1點新增了 20–30% P2P marketplace 的 take rate benchmark,口頭可以補一句「這是業界公開的參考區間」。第2點新增了「mechanism reference,不是 Cake 自己的抽成率」,這句要主動講,呼應 AmazingTalker 那頁的修正,避免面試官以為 Cake 真的要照抄 8–38% 那張表。第4點「怎麼不變成主線」口頭補充:教練媒合定位是履歷平台的加值延伸服務,不是把 Cake 重新定位成教練/家教平台;驗證階段由 Growth Ops 主導,不需要新設部門;對外溝通維持「求職平台」主軸。第5點「中文學校/教育單位」口頭補充:邏輯跟校園大使計畫一樣——這些機構的老師、留學顧問本來就有職涯輔導的量能,可以是教練來源;同時也是觸及東南亞/海外華語使用者的管道,呼應 Part3 第二階段的 inbound 國際線。六點不用每點都展開細講,這頁是「目錄式」陳述,讓面試官知道這個計畫禁得起從國際先例到在地執行的全面檢視,細節在後面幾頁。

Part 3 · Risk & Governance (raised proactively)

What I'd watch for, before anyone asks

Closest precedent: BloomTech (formerly Lambda School), misrepresented placement rates, sold students' future income shares to hedge funds, drew CFPB enforcement and class-action suits.

  • Explicit opt-in for referrals, never pre-checked.
  • Any external conversion claim needs real data behind it, never a story first, numbers later.
  • Placement/conversion rate tracked from day one (Launch Academy's 2024 enrolment pause is the cautionary case).
  • Coach vetting, phased by cost: career coaching isn't a licensed profession in Taiwan, so screening is designed, not assumed. Phase 1: interview, placement track record, and credentials where they exist, weighted toward coaches sourced through already-vetted networks (the same partner channels as the booth partners). Phase 2+: a checklist-gated onboarding, verified credentials required before a coach can accept a referral.
  • Stays a side-channel: an add-on to the resume platform, never the headline. If it ever competes with Cake's core identity for attention, that's a signal to slow down, not scale up.
這頁要主動講、不是等對方問。最後一條「怎麼不變成主線」呼應前面可驗證性框架的第4點,這裡用治理語言再講一次,強化這不只是口號,是一個有判斷標準的紀律。新增的教練品質把關這條,直接回應第21頁剛點出的104弱點(志工沒有品質把關)——完整版口頭補充見下方。
